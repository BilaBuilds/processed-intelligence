What We Deliver

Packages below ground. Standards above it.

ProcessEd is positioned for the kind of civils work where sequence, communication, and delivery quality matter as much as productivity on site.

01

Groundworks and foundations

Excavation, formation prep, substructure, foundations, concrete bases, and the groundwork required to move a project cleanly out of the ground.

Best for residential plots, commercial shells, school extensions, and enabling packages that need stable early-stage delivery.

02

Drainage and utilities

Foul and surface water drainage, attenuation systems, ducting, service coordination, and utility-related civils that keep the programme moving.

Ideal for schemes where below-ground coordination can either unlock the build or hold it back.

03

Enabling works and site preparation

Strip-out, clearance, remediation support, reduced level dig, and early works that create a reliable platform for the next trade in sequence.

Useful when principal contractors need a civils partner that can get sites ready without unnecessary drag.

04

External works and public realm support

Roads, kerbs, paving, hard landscaping, surfacing interfaces, and the civils details that turn a finished build into a finished place.

A strong fit for estate improvement works, public spaces, urban upgrades, and housing externals.

05

Principal contractor support

Responsive package delivery for project teams that need clean communication, reliable site reporting, and a civils contractor that integrates properly.

Designed for construction partners who want capability without the sluggishness that can come with bigger legacy setups.
