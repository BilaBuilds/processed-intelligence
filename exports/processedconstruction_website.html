What We Do

Our Services

End-to-end groundworks and civil engineering, from site investigation through to completion and handover.

⚒

Civil Engineering

Roads, bridges, retaining structures, and public realm infrastructure. Full design-and-build or construct-only packages for local authorities and government agencies.

⚙

Groundworks & Foundations

Site clearance, bulk excavation, piling, foundations, and substructure works. Everything below ground level so your build starts on solid footing.

💧

Drainage & Utilities

Foul and surface water drainage, attenuation systems, utility diversions and connections. NRSWA accredited for streetworks.

🌳

Landscaping & External Works

Hard and soft landscaping, car parks, fencing, footpaths, and public realm finishes. Delivered to council specification with full aftercare.

🛠

Site Preparation

Demolition, site strip, ground investigation, remediation, and enabling works. Getting complex sites ready for construction on programme.

🚧

Minor Works & Repairs

Reactive and planned minor civil works, repairs to highways, footpaths, and public assets. Framework-ready for term service contracts.

About Us

Built on Delivery,
Not Promises

ProcessEd Construction is a groundworks and civil engineering contractor serving public sector clients across London and the South East.

We specialise in the projects that councils and government bodies need done right the first time: drainage, foundations, site preparation, and infrastructure works. Every project is delivered with clear communication, transparent pricing, and a focus on programme certainty.

We actively monitor public sector procurement through Contracts Finder and Find a Tender, and we're set up to respond quickly to both framework call-offs and open tenders.
