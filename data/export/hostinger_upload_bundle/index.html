How it works

Your team is doing intelligence work manually.

ProcessEd turns repeated tender-monitoring work into a structured intelligence flow, so teams can see what is relevant, who is active, and when outreach is worth prioritising.

01

Portal monitoring

Public procurement feeds are monitored for new notices and metadata changes.

02

Deduplication

Repeated or overlapping notices are normalised into a cleaner opportunity stream.

03

Tender relevance filtering

Signals are scored against construction, civils, infrastructure, services, and timing cues.

04

Buyer pattern tracking

Buyer activity is grouped so recurring demand and warm accounts become easier to spot.

05

Timing signals

Deadline windows and fit scores are combined to surface actionable opportunities.
