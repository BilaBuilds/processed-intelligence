## Typical procurement patterns

- Frameworks refreshed every 4 years — watch for OJEU/FTS notices
- Mini-competitions run throughout framework life
- Direct awards at low values (£100k–£250k) via DA
- Public realm and landscaping often bundled with civils

## Key buyer types

- London boroughs (estates, highways, parks)
- Housing associations (planned maintenance programmes)
- NHS trusts (grounds, estates)
- Environment Agency (flood defence, river works)
- Highways England / National Highways (road works)

## Signal quality notes

- Buyers with quarterly or annual repeat patterns are most reliable
- Award notices 4–8 weeks before new procurement = strong forward signal
- Multiple award notices in same category = framework reprocurement coming
