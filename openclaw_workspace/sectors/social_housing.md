## Typical procurement patterns

- Annual planned maintenance programmes (kitchens, bathrooms, roofs, windows)
- Lot-based frameworks — often split by geography or trade
- DPS (Dynamic Purchasing Systems) increasingly common for reactive works
- Stock condition surveys precede large programmes by 6–12 months

## Key buyer types

- London boroughs housing departments
- Housing associations (G15 and regional)
- Arms-length management organisations (ALMOs)
- Homes England funded developers

## Signal quality notes

- Award clusters in Q1 often precede Q3–Q4 reprocurement
- Large single awards (>£2m) often followed by lot-based re-let
- Watch for "procurement strategy" or "market engagement" notices
