# 10-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-1:00 - Set The Problem

"Bid teams rarely lose time because one portal is impossible to search. They lose time because timing signals, buyer patterns, and fit signals are scattered. The result is late discovery, noisy shortlists, and too much manual triage before anyone can decide whether an opportunity is worth action."

"This demo shows a live Hostinger static dashboard using TenderNed JSON metadata. It is designed to validate whether ProcessEd can help a construction or civils team review public-sector opportunities faster. It is not an automated bid submission tool, and the VPS production pipeline is still unverified."

## 1:00-2:00 - Frame The Live Demo

"The dashboard is live on Hostinger, with no VPS, Flask, or DuckDB dependency for this demo. The current dataset is Dutch TenderNed metadata. The UK production pipeline is not part of this pilot unless verified later."

Point out:

- 50 Dutch tenders loaded.
- 21 shortlisted opportunities.
- 45 buyers tracked.
- Average fit score: 16 percent.

## 2:00-4:00 - Show The Dashboard

Walk through the main dashboard KPIs:

- Total tenders loaded.
- Shortlist count.
- Buyer count.
- Hot outreach items.
- Warm buyers.
- Timing-ready opportunities.

Talk track:

"This is the first-pass triage layer. The aim is not to replace bid judgement. The aim is to remove the first hour of hunting, filtering, and asking 'is there anything here worth looking at?'"

## 4:00-5:30 - Show Top Opportunities

Open the top opportunities area and explain:

- Opportunity title.
- Buyer.
- Deadline/timing.
- Fit score.
- Summary.
- Practical next step.

Talk track:

"The useful part is not just that a tender exists. It is the short explanation of why it may be relevant, what buyer is attached, and whether the timing is worth attention now."

## 5:30-6:45 - Show Hot Outreach And Timing-Ready Signals

Show:

- 5 hot outreach items.
- 7 timing-ready opportunities.
- Buyer warmth where available.

Talk track:

"For business development and framework teams, this is where the system becomes more interesting. It highlights where buyer timing or repeat activity may justify a human-reviewed contact or internal review."

## 6:45-8:15 - Ask Discovery Questions

Ask five questions:

1. Which sectors and work types should be included or excluded for your team?
2. Which regions or buyers matter most over the next quarter?
3. How do you currently decide whether a tender is worth review?
4. Where do timing signals usually get missed today?
5. What would a useful weekly summary need to contain for you to keep using it?

## 8:15-9:30 - Pilot Ask

"The sensible next step is a 2-week validation pilot. We use the live dashboard as the review surface, agree your target sectors and buyer types, and send a short weekly summary. The goal is to measure relevance, time saved, buyer insight usefulness, and whether the shortlist creates enough value to justify a paid continuation."

## 9:30-10:00 - Close

"If this looks useful, I suggest we pick one niche, one geography, and a small target buyer list. I will prepare the pilot tracker and first weekly review. At the end of two weeks, we decide from evidence whether it is worth continuing."

## Guardrails

- Do not claim guaranteed wins.
- Do not claim automated bid submission.
- Do not claim paying clients exist.
- Mention that the Hostinger dashboard is live.
- Mention that TenderNed JSON metadata is working.
- Mention that the VPS production pipeline is still unverified.
