# Pilot Success Metrics

## Measurement Goal

The 2-week pilot should answer one question: does the live Hostinger TenderNed dashboard help a construction, civils, infrastructure, or public-sector bid team make better opportunity decisions with less manual scanning?

The dashboard demo is live on Hostinger and currently uses TenderNed JSON metadata. The VPS production pipeline is still unverified, and the UK production pipeline is not part of this pilot unless verified later.

## Baseline Demo Metrics

- Tenders loaded: 50
- Shortlisted: 21
- Buyers tracked: 45
- Hot outreach items: 5
- Warm buyers: 19
- Timing-ready opportunities: 7
- Average fit score: 16 percent

These are demo baseline numbers, not production service-level guarantees.

## Metric 1 - Tender Relevance

Definition: percentage of shortlisted opportunities the pilot participant considers worth at least a quick review.

Target:

- Strong signal: 50 percent or more are relevant enough for review.
- Weak signal: below 25 percent are relevant after exclusion rules are applied.

Evidence to capture:

- Relevant / maybe / irrelevant classification.
- Reason for rejection.
- Missing sector, region, value, buyer, or timing criteria.

## Metric 2 - Time Saved

Definition: estimated time saved compared with manually scanning portals and buyer pages.

Target:

- Strong signal: at least 1-2 hours saved per week for a small bid or BD team.
- Weak signal: dashboard does not reduce manual scanning or decision time.

Evidence to capture:

- Current manual process.
- Time spent reviewing ProcessEd summary.
- Time normally spent finding equivalent opportunities.

## Metric 3 - Buyer Insight Usefulness

Definition: whether buyer tracking, warm buyer identification, and repeat buyer context help commercial decisions.

Target:

- Strong signal: participant identifies at least 3 buyers worth tracking.
- Weak signal: buyer context does not affect action or prioritisation.

Evidence to capture:

- Buyers marked warm, strategic, or ignore.
- Buyer notes requested by the participant.
- Any buyer pattern that changes follow-up priority.

## Metric 4 - Opportunity Discovery Quality

Definition: whether the pilot surfaces opportunities that are timely, actionable, and not obvious noise.

Target:

- Strong signal: at least 3 timing-ready opportunities or useful buyer signals in two weeks.
- Weak signal: opportunities are too generic, too late, or outside the participant's market.

Evidence to capture:

- Timing-ready opportunities reviewed.
- Opportunity selected for action.
- Opportunity rejected because deadline, scope, or location did not fit.

## Metric 5 - Demo-To-Pilot Conversion

Definition: percentage of qualified demos that become a 2-week pilot.

Target:

- Early validation target: 20-30 percent of qualified demos convert to pilot.

Notes:

- This is a validation target only.
- Do not describe it as current achieved performance unless measured later.

## Metric 6 - Pilot-To-Paid Conversion

Definition: percentage of completed pilots that become a paid subscription, paid report, or paid weekly intelligence workflow.

Target:

- Early validation target: 25-50 percent of completed pilots where relevance and time saved are strong.

Notes:

- Do not claim paying clients exist.
- Record reasons for no-conversion: relevance, budget, timing, trust, missing UK data, missing workflow integration, or unclear ROI.

## Pass / Continue / Stop Decision

Pass:

- Shortlist relevance is credible.
- At least one meaningful buyer or timing insight is found.
- The participant says the weekly summary would save time.
- There is a specific paid continuation path.

Continue:

- Feedback is positive but targeting needs refinement.
- Participant wants UK data verification before paid use.
- Buyer tracking is useful, but opportunity scoring needs tuning.

Stop:

- Shortlists are irrelevant after tuning.
- Timing and buyer signals do not change decisions.
- Participant does not see a workflow where this saves time or improves action.
