# ProcessEd V3 Board Report
Date: 2026-04-01  
Audience: Founders, investors, institutional buyers, strategic partners  
Positioning doctrine: Category leader, not alert tool

## Executive Summary

Public procurement software is moving into a new control cycle. The previous era was dominated by discovery products (more notices, more alerts, more search). The next era will be defined by decision infrastructure: systems that convert fragmented public-market records into explainable decisions, verified risk signals, and audit-grade evidence.

ProcessEd should be positioned as:

> The decision infrastructure for public procurement.

That framing is commercially and structurally correct for four reasons:

1. Data volume has scaled, but decision quality has not.
2. Buyers and suppliers are optimizing for triage certainty, not alert volume.
3. Compliance and accountability obligations are increasing.
4. Existing competitors are still specialized by function, not converged by workflow.

The UK regime is now materially supportive of this strategy. Procurement Act changes in force since 24 February 2025 and related guidance increase the importance of structured transparency, compliance controls, exclusions/debarment pathways, and evidential defensibility.  
Source: [GOV.UK supplier guide](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/67b5f15ebd116e3d7b1cf2d4/20250224_Act_Now_A_guide_for_suppliers_A4_v2.0_FINAL.pdf)

The addressable gravity remains large and persistent. OECD reports public procurement expenditure at 12.7% of GDP across OECD countries and 14.8% across OECD-EU contexts.  
Source: [OECD Government at a Glance 2025](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/government-at-a-glance-2025_0efd0bcd-en/full-report/size-of-public-procurement_6979cd47.html)

The category winner is unlikely to be the platform with the largest notice index. It is likely to be the platform with the strongest procurement truth graph, best decision explainability, and highest trust under scrutiny.

## 1) Category Thesis

ProcessEd is not a tender feed, bid helper, or generic AI assistant.

It is a converged decision layer across:

- public procurement data infrastructure
- deterministic and explainable decision intelligence
- compliance and verification surfaces

This convergence creates one underlying platform with four commercial surfaces:

1. SME bid triage and conversion.
2. Legal/investigative evidence workflows.
3. Institutional supplier-risk and oversight intelligence.
4. API/data infrastructure licensing.

## 2) Why This Category Exists Now

### Structural trigger A: Data exists, operational utility does not

Public portals and notices are improving, but operational usefulness is still limited by inconsistency, duplication, and workflow mismatch for SMEs and operators.

### Structural trigger B: Compliance is becoming a product requirement

Transparency, exclusions, debarment logic, and contract performance visibility now affect not only policy but software trust, buyer confidence, and purchasing decisions.

### Structural trigger C: AI alone is not a moat

Generic AI can draft narratives. It cannot replace normalized data contracts, deterministic scoring, provenance links, and repeatable operational outcomes.

## 3) Market Opportunity by Lens

### Lens A: SME commercial

Primary need:
- Should we spend two weeks bidding this?

Required output:
- explainable BID/REVIEW/NO_BID
- high-signal shortlist
- risk and deadline context

### Lens B: Legal / investigative / anti-corruption

Primary need:
- Can this procurement decision be challenged, defended, or investigated with evidence?

Required output:
- supplier linkage patterns
- anomaly/risk signals
- provenance-ready case trail

### Lens C: Investable technology asset

Primary need:
- Is this architecture compounding and defensible?

Required output:
- canonical interfaces
- data quality and freshness discipline
- decision logs + outcome feedback loops

### Lens D: Institutional public-sector intelligence

Primary need:
- Which supplier risks are becoming visible before failure or challenge?

Required output:
- supplier-risk overlays
- performance/exclusion context
- auditable decision support

## 4) Strategic Positioning (Board Decision)

Do not position as:

- tender alerts
- procurement AI assistant
- opportunity scraper

Position as:

> Decision intelligence for public procurement.

Strategic sequence:

1. SME-first revenue engine (cash and iteration speed).
2. Institutional risk and assurance overlays (higher ACV).
3. Procurement graph API/data products (defensibility and platform valuation).

## 5) Competitive Gap to Exploit

Incumbents are generally strong in one domain and weak across domains:

- discovery-first tools: broad but noisy
- analytics suites: strong dashboards, weak operator triage
- compliance/risk systems: deep controls, poor SME workflow fit
- investigative platforms: strong graph research, weak recurring commercial operations

ProcessEd's category edge comes from joining these domains around one decision spine.

## 6) Procurement Truth Graph Doctrine

All products must inherit from five canonical entities:

1. `ProcurementOpportunity`
2. `SupplierEntity`
3. `DecisionEvent`
4. `RiskSignal`
5. `LeadActivationRecord`

If a feature does not strengthen one of these entities, their links, or conversion outcomes, it is deprioritized.

## 7) 12-Month North Star

By month 12, ProcessEd should prove:

1. Revenue truth: repeatable SME pilot-to-paid conversion.
2. Evidence truth: institutional and forensic outputs reproducible from provenance and decision logs.
3. Platform truth: graph and API readiness for higher-value recurring contracts.

This is the shortest path from "useful product" to "category infrastructure."

## 8) Build Mandate

Immediate mandate:

1. Keep deterministic decision quality as the product contract.
2. Expand provenance and risk-signal surfaces.
3. Compound the graph while monetizing SME workflow.
4. Scale only what strengthens decision trust and conversion.

Operating test for every new feature:

> Does this improve procurement truth, decision explainability, or commercial conversion?

If no, it waits.
