# ProcessEd 7-Day Outreach Playbook
Date: 2026-04-01
Owner: Bilal
Use with: `PROCESS_ED_OUTREACH_PROMPT_CLAUDE.md` and `PROCESS_ED_OUTREACH_PROMPT_CHATGPT.md`

## Objective

Book qualified calls for a 7-day ProcessEd pilot and convert accepted pilots into paid monthly users.

## Core Offer

- Offer: 7-day pilot
- Promise: 5 high-fit live tenders per week + BID/REVIEW/NO_BID + confidence + risk flags
- Audience: SME contractors and subcontractors

## Daily Operator Loop

1. Build today's list (20 to 30 prospects).
2. Send that day's sequence message.
3. Log all sends and replies.
4. Process responses using objection scripts.
5. Set call slots and send confirmations.
6. End day with KPI snapshot.

## Day-by-Day Sequence

### Day 1 - First Touch

Message:

```text
Hi [Name], quick one.
I run ProcessEd, a tender intelligence feed for [trade] firms in [region].
We send live opportunities already filtered with BID/REVIEW/NO_BID notes, so your team avoids low-fit bids.
I am offering a 7-day pilot this week. Want a sample shortlist for your area?
If not relevant, reply STOP and I will close the loop.
```

### Day 2 - Follow-up

Message:

```text
Hi [Name], circling back.
I can send 2 sample opportunities matched to [trade]/[region] so you can judge quality before deciding.
Worth a look?
If not relevant, reply STOP and I will close the loop.
```

### Day 3 - Value Drop

Message:

```text
Hi [Name], here is one example of what we send:
- Tender: [title]
- Fit: [why fit]
- Decision: [BID/REVIEW]
If this is the type of work you want more of, I can activate your 7-day pilot.
If not relevant, reply STOP and I will close the loop.
```

### Day 4 - Call Ask

Message:

```text
Hi [Name], useful to do a 12-minute fit call.
I will map your trade, region, and value band and show exactly what your pilot feed would look like.
Would [option 1] or [option 2] work?
If not relevant, reply STOP and I will close the loop.
```

### Day 5 - Objection Day

Price objection:

```text
Fair point.
Let us run the 7-day pilot first and judge only on relevance.
If quality is poor, stop immediately.
```

Timing objection:

```text
Understood.
This is designed to save time by pre-qualifying tenders before your team sees them.
We can run the pilot with minimal lift from your side.
```

Existing provider objection:

```text
That makes sense.
Use us as a parallel benchmark for 7 days and compare signal quality directly.
If we do not add value, no need to continue.
```

### Day 6 - Final Nudge

Message:

```text
Last follow-up from me, [Name].
If better-fit tenders are still a priority this month, I can hold one pilot slot for [trade] in [region].
Want me to reserve it?
If not relevant, reply STOP and I will close the loop.
```

### Day 7 - Close or Park

Close:

```text
Great, to activate your pilot send:
- Trade focus
- Region(s)
- Ideal contract value range
You will get first matches within 24 hours.
```

Park:

```text
No pressure, I will close this out for now.
If priorities change, message "pilot" and I will reopen a slot.
```

## KPI Targets

- Daily sends: 20 to 30
- Reply rate target: 8% to 15%
- Positive reply target: 3% to 7%
- Booked call target: 1 to 3 per day once volume stabilizes
- Pilot conversion target: 20%+ of qualified calls

## Daily Tracker Fields

- Date
- Prospect name
- Company
- Trade
- Region
- Message day sent (D1 to D7)
- Response status (none, replied, positive, stop)
- Call booked (yes/no)
- Pilot started (yes/no)
- Outcome notes

## Compliance Guardrails

1. Identify sender clearly.
2. Do not make legal or fraud accusations in outreach.
3. Include opt-out language in every outbound message.
4. Stop contacting immediately if recipient opts out.
5. Keep records of sends, opt-outs, and responses.

## Weekly Review

At end of week:

1. Review message conversion by day and variant.
2. Remove weak variants and keep top performers.
3. Tighten audience targeting by trade and region.
4. Refresh sample tender examples for relevance.
5. Set next week's volume and conversion goals.
