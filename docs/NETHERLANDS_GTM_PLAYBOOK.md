# Netherlands GTM Playbook

Status: demo go-to-market playbook. It uses sample opportunity patterns and should not be treated as verified live market coverage.

## Target Segments

1. UK and EU civil engineering contractors exploring Benelux public works.
2. Drainage, water, pumping, and flood-resilience specialists.
3. Road maintenance, surfacing, bridge inspection, and traffic management suppliers.
4. Engineering consultants and bid teams that need Dutch-to-English qualification support.
5. Product suppliers for municipal, transport, port, and water authority frameworks.

## Core Offer

Netherlands procurement intelligence for infrastructure teams:

- English summaries of Dutch public works opportunities.
- CPV and buyer-category routing.
- Fit and urgency scoring.
- Cross-border opportunity tagging from EU/TED notices.
- Weekly target-account recommendations.

## Qualification Questions

- Which Dutch regions or buyer categories are commercially reachable?
- Can the prospect bid directly, or do they need a local partner?
- Which CPV groups match their existing case studies?
- Do they need live alerts, weekly reports, or one-off market mapping?
- Are they targeting works, consultancy, products, or maintenance frameworks?

## Pilot Package

Run a four-week Netherlands pilot:

1. Agree 5-10 CPV groups and 20 named buyer categories or regions.
2. Produce a weekly opportunity dashboard and short written digest.
3. Flag cross-border/TED opportunities separately from local municipal notices.
4. Add buyer recurrence notes and recommended qualification action.
5. End with a market-entry map and a prioritized target-account list.

## Demo Caveats

All records in the demo bundle are sanitized sample records. They are designed to prove product shape, not to represent live tender coverage. Before using this commercially, replace fixtures with verified source records, add source attribution, and review source terms.
