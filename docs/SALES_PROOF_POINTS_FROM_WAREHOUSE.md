# Sales Proof Points From Warehouse

These proof points are based on historical local warehouse analysis from generated, client-safe exports. They are designed for cautious sales conversations and pilot materials.

## Verified Counts

- 15,786 historical tender rows.
- 10,325 historical award rows.
- 2,274 buyer memory rows.
- 201 run log rows.

## Strongest Safe Proof Points

- ProcessEd can show repeat-buyer concentration rather than only fresh tender alerts.
- The warehouse identifies high-volume public buyers such as Buckinghamshire Council - e-Tendering System, Department for Education, NHS England, Ministry of Defence, and UK Research & Innovation (UKRI).
- Buyer memory makes it possible to discuss historical procurement behaviour at account level.
- Monthly trend exports show warehouse coverage over time and can support a dashboard narrative.
- Recent high-score tender examples can be used as demo evidence, with live validation before any commercial action.

## Sales Language To Use

- "We have historical local warehouse analysis across 15,786 tender rows and 10,325 award rows."
- "We can turn historical procurement records into buyer shortlists and briefing material."
- "This helps prioritise which buyers are worth researching first."
- "This is evidence for a pilot workflow, not a claim of full production automation."

## Sales Language To Avoid

- Do not say ProcessEd has paying clients unless separately verified.
- Do not present tender wins as assured.
- Do not present the workflow as live production automation.
- Do not say award-winner supplier outreach is ready from this export; current supplier data is not safe enough for Hermes outreach.
- Do not imply raw warehouse files will be shared with prospects, Hermes, or dashboards.

## Demo Narrative

1. Start with the verified warehouse counts.
2. Show the top buyer concentration.
3. Show one recent high-score example as historical evidence.
4. Explain that buyer reports and weekly briefings can be generated from curated exports.
5. End with the next validation step: pick a contractor niche, map fit against top buyers, and validate live tender status before action.

## Compliance Boundary

This pack includes only derived CSV and Markdown files. It does not include raw DB files, credentials, logs, or unsafe Hermes outreach targets.
