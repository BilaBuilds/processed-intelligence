# DACH Scoring Model

This DACH scoring layer is heuristic and exists for demo prioritisation only. It works only on local fixture data generated by the DACH demo export layer.

No live scraping has been performed. There is no live DACH coverage claim, no paid client claim, no guaranteed win claim, and this is not bid advice.

`run_pipeline.py` was intentionally not modified. The current TenderNed/Hostinger dashboard remains untouched.

## Scoring Inputs

The scorer reads `data/dach/demo/dach_demo_tenders.json` and writes:

- `data/dach/demo/dach_demo_scored.json`
- `data/dach/demo/dach_demo_score_summary.json`

The output remains local fixture-only demo data.

## Heuristic Signals

Scoring version `dach_fixture_scoring_v0_2` separates major infrastructure/civils signals from construction/building works signals so the fixture demo can show a realistic priority spread without implying live DACH coverage.

Major infrastructure/civils signals include drainage, road, bridge, groundworks, civil engineering, utilities, wastewater, and infrastructure. These add stronger positive weight because they are closest to the construction/civils use case.

Construction/building works signals include concrete, maintenance, refurbishment, public building, school, school refurbishment, framework, term contract, repair, construction, and building works.

Negative or non-core signals include pure IT, software only, office supplies, consulting only, vehicles only, medical equipment, legal services, catering, and cleaning only.

## Timing Rules

- Expired or stale notices are marked `MONITOR` with the reason `Expired/stale notice` and next action `Monitor only`.
- Deadlines within 14 days receive `Urgent deadline` and a smaller urgency boost.
- Deadlines 15-45 days away receive `Timing-ready review window` and the strongest timing boost.
- Deadlines beyond 45 days receive `Watchlist timing`.

## Value Rules

- Missing value is not penalised and receives `Value not published`.
- Values at or above 1000000 receive `High-value opportunity`.
- Values at or above 250000 receive `Material contract value`.
- Smaller relevant works receive `Relevant smaller works`.

## Priority Rules

- `HIGH`: fit score at or above 70 and not expired.
- `MEDIUM`: fit score at or above 45 and not expired.
- `LOW`: fit score above 0 and not expired.
- `MONITOR`: expired, stale, or score 0.

## Action Labels

These are workflow labels for demo prioritisation, not bid advice.

- `HIGH`: Review for pilot shortlist.
- `MEDIUM`: Review if geography and capacity fit.
- `LOW`: Keep on watchlist.
- `MONITOR`: Monitor only.
