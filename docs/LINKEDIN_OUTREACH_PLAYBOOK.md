# LinkedIn Outreach Playbook

## Purpose

Use LinkedIn manually to start relevant conversations with bid, preconstruction, commercial, framework, and business development leaders in UK/NL construction, civils, infrastructure, and public-sector supplier teams.

This playbook is for copy-paste outreach only. It does not automate sending, does not require Apollo, does not require internet access, and does not claim guaranteed tender wins.

## Positioning

Lead with a practical problem:

Bid teams miss useful timing signals and buyer patterns because public-sector opportunity data is scattered across portals, buyer pages, and spreadsheets.

Then introduce the demo:

ProcessEd has a live Hostinger static dashboard using TenderNed JSON metadata. The demo currently has 50 Dutch tenders loaded, 21 shortlisted, 45 buyers tracked, 5 hot outreach items, 19 warm buyers, and 7 timing-ready opportunities.

Important caveat:

The VPS production pipeline is still unverified. The UK production pipeline is not part of this pilot unless verified later.

## Target Personas

- Bid Manager
- Preconstruction Lead
- Commercial Director
- Framework Manager
- Business Development Director

## Manual Sequence

### 1. Connection Request Under 300 Characters

Hi {{first_name}}, I am testing a tender intelligence dashboard for construction and civils bid teams. It helps spot shortlisted opportunities, buyer patterns, and timing signals. Thought it may be relevant to your role.

### 2. Follow-Up After Acceptance

Thanks for connecting, {{first_name}}. I am running a small 2-week validation pilot for ProcessEd. The live demo is a Hostinger dashboard using TenderNed metadata, with 50 tenders loaded, 21 shortlisted, and 45 buyers tracked.

### 3. Value Message

The idea is simple: reduce manual portal scanning by turning tender metadata into a shortlist, buyer watchlist, and timing-ready opportunity view. It is not automated bid submission or a promise of tender wins. It is a faster review layer for human bid judgement.

### 4. Demo Ask

Would a 15-20 minute walkthrough be useful? I can show the live dashboard, the top opportunities, and how a 2-week pilot would measure relevance, time saved, and buyer insight usefulness.

### 5. Soft Close

No pressure if this is not a current priority. I can either leave it here or send a short example of the weekly summary format so you can judge whether it is useful later.

## Qualification Notes

Good-fit signals:

- Mentions bids, tenders, preconstruction, frameworks, public sector, infrastructure, civils, highways, drainage, housing, utilities, or procurement.
- Works in UK/NL construction or adjacent infrastructure.
- Responsible for finding, qualifying, or winning public-sector work.

Weak-fit signals:

- Pure private-sector sales role with no tender responsibility.
- Consultancy, software, HR, legal, catering, cleaning, or security unless there is a clear infrastructure angle.
- Individual consumer profile or no business relevance.

## Compliance Guardrails

- Send manually only.
- Personalise lightly from public profile context.
- Do not scrape private data.
- Do not imply a relationship that does not exist.
- Do not claim paying clients exist.
- Do not promise tender wins.
- Do not claim the VPS pipeline is verified.
