# ProcessEd — Pitch Video Script
**"Decision Intelligence for Public Procurement"**
Duration: 60–90 seconds | Style: Cinematic B2B | Tone: Sharp, credible, ambitious

---

## SHOT 1 — THE PAIN (0:00–0:15)

**Visual:** Split screen. Left: a contractor at a desk, late evening, buried in browser tabs — Contracts Finder, email, spreadsheets, PDF notices. Right: a stream of raw procurement data scrolling fast — titles, deadlines, values, duplicates, noise. Overwhelming. No structure.

**On-screen text (fade in):**
> "Every day, the UK publishes thousands of procurement notices."

**Voiceover:**
> "UK construction SMEs are drowning in procurement data. Too many notices. No way to know what's worth pursuing."

---

## SHOT 2 — THE COST (0:15–0:25)

**Visual:** Close-up of a bid team reviewing a tender — hours of work, spreadsheets, phone calls. Then a screen showing the result: *Contract Awarded — Different Supplier.* Time wasted. Money lost.

**On-screen text:**
> "Wasted bids. Missed deadlines. Opportunities that never got a look."

**Voiceover:**
> "Contractors spend weeks on bids they never should have touched — and miss the ones they could have won."

---

## SHOT 3 — THE TRANSITION (0:25–0:40)

**Visual:** Dark screen. Then — a clean interface emerges. Data flowing in from multiple sources. Normalised. Scored. Filtered. A decision engine activating: green BID tags, amber REVIEW tags, grey NO-BID. Confidence scores. Risk flags. Regional fit indicators. Deadline clarity. All moving with precision.

**On-screen text (sequential):**
> "Ingested." → "Scored." → "Decided."

**Voiceover:**
> "ProcessEd ingests live public procurement data, scores every opportunity against your trade and region, and delivers a daily ranked shortlist — with clear BID, REVIEW, or NO-BID decisions."

---

## SHOT 4 — THE PRODUCT (0:40–0:55)

**Visual:** Discord channel on screen — clean embed notification arriving. Top 5 tenders. Each one showing: title, buyer, region (readable — "South East England"), value, deadline, verdict ("BID — 87% confidence"), reason codes ("construction match, value band strong, deadline 18 days"), risk flags ("tight timeline — act today"). One contractor glances at their phone, nods, opens a tender link. Confident. Focused. No noise.

**On-screen text:**
> "Right tenders. Right time. Right decision."

**Voiceover:**
> "No more searching. No more guessing. Just the opportunities worth pursuing — with the reasoning to back every recommendation."

---

## SHOT 5 — THE CATEGORY (0:55–1:05)

**Visual:** Pull back to show the system at scale. Multiple regions lighting up on a UK map. Decision events logging. Audit trails forming. A simple dashboard — pipeline healthy, 9 new matches, 2 BID verdicts, compliance clear. One line of text crystallises on screen.

**Voiceover:**
> "Built for the firms that build Britain."

**On-screen text (bold, centred, full screen):**
> **ProcessEd**
> *Decision intelligence for public procurement.*

---

## END FRAME

**Logo + tagline lockup:**
> ProcessEd — Decision intelligence for public procurement.

**Optional CTA line:**
> processeduk.com | Early access open now

## NOTES FOR PRODUCTION

- No stock footage of generic "AI" visuals (neural networks, floating data orbs). Keep it grounded in real contractor work and real interface elements.
- The Discord embed is a real product surface — show it accurately.
- BID/REVIEW/NO-BID colour coding: green / amber / grey. Never red — this is a decision tool, not an alarm system.
- Voiceover tone: calm, authoritative, British. Not salesy.
- Music: minimal, ambient, slightly industrial. Builds quietly through shot 3, resolves cleanly on end frame.
