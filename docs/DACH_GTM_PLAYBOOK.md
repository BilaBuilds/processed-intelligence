# DACH GTM Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook supports a ProcessEd Intelligence DACH procurement intelligence pilot for construction and civils teams. The current DACH demo is fixture-only and pilot/discovery stage. It is not a live DACH feed, and no automation claim should be made until sources are verified.

## Market Order

1. Germany
2. Switzerland
3. Austria

## Why This Order

Germany is first because it is the largest DACH public procurement and construction opportunity market.

Switzerland is second because it is strong for premium buyer intelligence and has a high-value contractor market.

Austria is third because it is a smaller but useful German-language extension once Germany and Switzerland positioning is working.

## ICP

- Construction/civils contractors
- Infrastructure contractors
- Maintenance contractors
- Public works suppliers
- Engineering contractors
- Utilities maintenance suppliers

## Target Roles

- Geschäftsführer
- Vertriebsleiter
- Leiter Ausschreibungen
- Ausschreibungsmanagement
- Kalkulation
- Angebotsmanagement
- Business Development
- Bid Manager
- Commercial Director
- Preconstruction Lead

## Offer

- DACH procurement intelligence pilot
- Buyer watchlist
- Tender shortlisting
- Timing signal review
- Weekly opportunity report

## First Pilot Shape

- 2-week pilot
- Fixture/demo walkthrough first
- Then manually verified source review
- Weekly opportunity report
- 15-minute review call
- No automation claim until sources are verified

## Positioning

ProcessEd helps construction and civils teams turn public procurement notices into ranked opportunities, buyer watchlists, and timing-ready review actions.

## Commercial Warnings

- Do not present DACH coverage as live yet.
- No win-rate guarantee.
- No bid-advice language.
- No paying-client claim.
- No fully automated source claim.
- Keep language honest: fixture-only DACH demo, pilot/discovery stage.

## First Outreach Motion

Start with Germany infrastructure contractors and public works suppliers, then test Switzerland civil engineering and utility suppliers, then extend to Austria construction/civils contractors.

The outreach ask should be a 15-minute feedback call, not a sales claim. The message should say the team is testing a DACH procurement intelligence pilot and would value feedback from bid, commercial, and preconstruction teams.
