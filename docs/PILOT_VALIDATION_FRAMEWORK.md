# ProcessEd 2-Week Pilot Validation Framework

## Objective

Validate whether the live ProcessEd Hostinger TenderNed dashboard can help UK/NL construction, civils, infrastructure, and public-sector bid teams find relevant tender opportunities faster, understand buyer timing signals earlier, and decide which opportunities deserve follow-up.

This is a demo-led pilot, not a claim of production automation. The current demo uses TenderNed JSON metadata, the Hostinger static dashboard is live, and the VPS production pipeline is still unverified. The UK production pipeline is not part of this pilot unless it is verified later and explicitly added to scope.

## Ideal Customer Profile

The pilot is designed for:

- UK/NL construction contractors
- Civils and infrastructure firms
- Public-sector bid teams
- Preconstruction teams
- Framework and business development teams tracking public buyers

Best-fit companies already monitor public-sector opportunities and want a faster way to spot timing, fit, buyer patterns, and early engagement opportunities.

## Buyer Personas

- Bid Manager: needs faster bid/no-bid triage and fewer missed deadlines.
- Preconstruction Lead: needs early visibility of work that fits capability and region.
- Commercial Director: wants a short list of credible opportunities without dashboard noise.
- Framework Manager: tracks buyers, frameworks, repeat patterns, and route-to-market clues.
- Business Development Director: wants warm buyer signals and timely reasons to start a conversation.

## What The Pilot Delivers

- Live tender dashboard review using the Hostinger static dashboard.
- Review of the current TenderNed demo data: 50 Dutch tenders loaded, 21 shortlisted, and 45 buyers tracked.
- Shortlisted opportunities with scores, summaries, and practical next-step notes.
- Buyer tracking for repeat or warm public-sector buyers.
- Timing-ready opportunity list for tenders where deadline or recency makes action sensible.
- Weekly summary/report covering relevance, buyer insight, timing, and next actions.
- Manual outreach support material for human-reviewed follow-up.

## Current Demo Evidence

- Hostinger dashboard is live.
- TenderNed JSON data is working.
- 50 Dutch tenders are loaded.
- 21 opportunities are shortlisted.
- 45 buyers are tracked.
- 5 hot outreach items are visible.
- 19 warm buyers are visible.
- 7 timing-ready opportunities are visible.
- Average fit score is currently 16 percent.
- Top opportunities are scored and summarised.
- There is no VPS, Flask, or DuckDB dependency for the live static demo.

## Out Of Scope

- No automated bid submission.
- No guaranteed tender wins.
- No legal advice or procurement advice.
- No full CRM integration yet.
- No automated LinkedIn sending.
- No automated email sending without human review.
- No claim that UK production ingestion is active in this pilot.
- No reliance on VPS production pipeline until separately verified.

## Pilot Structure

Week 1:

- 30-minute onboarding and discovery call.
- Confirm target sectors, regions, buyer types, and exclusion criteria.
- Review the live dashboard and explain the TenderNed metadata basis.
- Select example opportunities and buyer patterns to validate.
- Deliver the first short summary with relevance and timing observations.

Week 2:

- Review the second batch of findings.
- Capture feedback on relevance, time saved, buyer usefulness, and missed categories.
- Refine fit rules manually from feedback.
- Agree whether a paid continuation is justified.

## Validation Questions

- Did the dashboard surface tenders the team would otherwise have missed?
- Were shortlisted opportunities relevant enough to review?
- Did buyer tracking reveal useful patterns?
- Did timing-ready signals make outreach or bid review easier?
- Did the weekly summary save meaningful manual scanning time?
- What would need to be true for this to become a paid workflow?

## Compliance And Positioning Guardrails

- Present this as a 2-week validation pilot, not a mature procurement automation platform.
- Say the Hostinger static dashboard is live.
- Say TenderNed JSON metadata is working.
- Say the VPS production pipeline is still unverified.
- Do not claim paying clients exist.
- Do not promise tender outcomes.
- Do not imply the system submits bids or gives procurement/legal advice.
